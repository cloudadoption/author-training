# Experience Workspace author guide

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

New and occasional content authors learning to create, edit, preview, review,
and publish pages with Experience Workspace.

## Product Purpose

Provide task-based training and site-specific reference material, so authors
can complete the edit–preview–publish workflow confidently.

## Operating Context

The existing Edge Delivery Services repository serves code. DA at
https://da.live/#/cloudadoption/author-training holds separately published content.
This implementation provides import-ready content; uploading and publishing are
not authorized.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Use the existing vanilla JavaScript/CSS blocks and document-oriented authoring.
- No production build or runtime dependencies.
- Keep lessons editable in DA, not embedded in JavaScript.
- Preview is not an access-control boundary.
- Assistant, asset integrations, permissions, and libraries depend on site setup.
- Do not invent organization-specific approval policies or available integrations.

## Brand Commitments

Adobe-branded documentation with topic navigation and readable lessons, as
confirmed by the user. The functional reference is the RBC author guide at
https://main--rbc--aemsites.aem.page/docs/enablement/.
Use plain, encouraging language and distinguish this training site from official
Adobe product documentation.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing block implementations and official Experience Workspace authoring
documentation at https://www.aem.live/docs/ew/authoring.
No approved Adobe logo asset or organization-specific screenshots were supplied.
