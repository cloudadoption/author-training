Govern publishing

Make the difference between preparation, review, scheduling, and publication explicit. Enabling a menu item is not the same as enforcing an organization-wide approval policy.

Configure the Prepare menu

The ellipsis Prepare menu hosts actions for documents, sheets, and media before preview or publish. Configure it through a prepare tab in the organization or site settings.

  • Always-on: Unpublish is available independently of optional feature configuration.
  • Adobe feature flags: entries such as Schedule Publish and Send to Adobe Target are enabled by their documented title.
  • Project plugins: custom actions need a plugin path and any additional setup their integration requires.

The optional ref field can limit visibility during testing. Treat visibility as rollout control, not a substitute for service authorization. Check inheritance and avoid overriding a built-in action accidentally with a same-title plugin.

Understand Preflight's enforcement boundary

Preflight runs Governance Agent checks from the tools panel. Authors can inspect passed, failed, and not-applicable results, view suggestions, fix issues, and rerun checks.

The configuration key editor.enforcePreflight set to true requires a passing check for document publishing through the editor. Editing the document requires a new check. The setting is off by default and does not affect Preview or bulk publishing.

Define which publication paths your governance policy covers. Do not describe this setting as a universal gate on every publish route, and do not assume a pass replaces human review.

Set up Request Publish

  1. Arrange onboarding with your Adobe contact for the organization and site, allowed email domains, and your notification service. Exchange credentials through the approved private channel.
  2. Configure the author-facing Request Publish plugin and the Publish Requests inbox app. Use Prepare when the workflow must include media; choose the appropriate editor extension for a pages-only workflow.
  3. Define path-based approver rules in the publish-workflow-config tab, plus optional distribution-list mappings and workflow settings.
  4. Give authors the required access to the workflow request sheet and the appropriate Edge Delivery author role. Approvers need the required publish role.
  5. Test submission, notification, diff review, rejection, withdrawal, and successful approval on a practice page.

Approval in this workflow publishes the content. Verify the resulting live URL; a notification email alone is not publication evidence. Decide whether CC recipients may approve rather than assuming they can. For assistant-driven requests, also enable the connector and skill described in assistant workflows.

Enable and operate scheduled publication

  1. Add the Schedule Publish entry to the Prepare configuration, optionally limiting its initial audience.
  2. Open the Scheduler app, load the organization/site, and register the site if required.
  3. On a practice document or sheet, schedule a publication and verify the intended date, time, and time zone.
  4. Review the schedule list and agree who will monitor changes before the scheduled time.

A schedule publishes content as it exists at the scheduled time, not an immutable copy captured when scheduling. Individual scheduling automatically previews the content. The Scheduler app's Delete control cancels a schedule without a confirmation dialog, so use it carefully.

Validate the whole workflow

Test as an author and approver, verify their separate permissions, and check both preview and live URLs. Include failure and cancellation cases in your rollout plan. Return to the author checklist for the everyday publication steps.
