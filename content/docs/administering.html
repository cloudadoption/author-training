Administer Experience Workspace

Set up a workspace that helps authors create confidently and publish responsibly. This guide is for site administrators and the developers or operations teams who support them.

These are training guides, not changes to this site's configuration. Features, licensing, onboarding, and permissions vary by organization. Check the linked Adobe documentation before applying a setting.

Start with a safe change

  1. Confirm the organization, site, environment, and administrator responsible for the change.
  2. Record the current configuration and agree on a rollback plan.
  3. Test in a practice site or with a limited audience before a wider rollout.
  4. Verify the result as an ordinary author, not only as an administrator.
  5. Document what changed and tell authors what they can now do.
