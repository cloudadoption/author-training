Preview and publish safely

Saving, previewing, and publishing are separate actions. Know which version your reviewer is seeing before you make a change live.

Know the three states

  • Draft: the content you are editing in Experience Workspace. Saving does not update the preview or live site.
  • Preview: the review copy at an aem.page URL. It is not your production website, but it may be publicly reachable. Do not use it to protect sensitive information.
  • Live: the published content at an aem.live URL or configured production domain. Treat publishing as a public release.

For this site, preview starts at https://main--author-training--cloudadoption.aem.page/ and live at https://main--author-training--cloudadoption.aem.live/. The page path follows the host.

Run the preflight checklist

  • Copy is accurate, complete, and approved by the appropriate owner.
  • The page has a clear title, description, and logical heading structure.
  • Links work and point to intended destinations, not editor or accidental preview URLs.
  • Images load, alternative text is appropriate, and important content works on mobile.
  • New linked pages and shared fragments are ready to preview and publish.
  • No placeholder text, private data, or training-only examples remain.
  • The latest preview, not an earlier draft, has been reviewed.

These are recommended checks, not a configured approval gate. Use your organization's actual approval requirements. If automated preflight checks are enabled, run those too.

Update the preview

  1. Wait for your draft to finish saving.
  2. Open the editor's Send menu, select Preview, then Update.
  3. Inspect the opened preview URL and share it for review.
  4. If you make more edits, preview again so the reviewer sees them.

Publish after approval

  1. Confirm you have publishing permission and the required approval.
  2. Preview and publish any new shared content or destination pages needed by this page.
  3. In the Send menu, choose Preview & Publish, check the destination, and select Update. In the content browser, select the page and use its Publish action instead.
  4. Open the live URL separately. Verify the intended change, key links, and media.

The Sidekick offers preview and publish actions on viewed pages when configured. If an action is unavailable, check your permissions; do not repeatedly retry a potentially live action.

If something goes wrong

Coordinate with the site owner. Restore a known good version, preview it, check it, and publish it when authorized. Unpublishing is a separate, disruptive action: it removes both preview and live URLs. Do not delete or unpublish a page as a routine way to fix a typo.
