Create your first page

Make a small practice page, preview it, and check the result. You do not need to publish it to complete this lesson.

Choose a safe place to practice

Ask your site owner which folder is approved for training. A folder named drafts is a convention, not a guarantee of privacy. Do not add confidential, personal, or unreleased material to a publicly reachable preview.

Create a page

  1. In the content browser, navigate to the approved training folder.
  2. Select New, then Page. Enter a short, descriptive name such as my-first-page and select Create.
  3. In the editor, add one Heading 1, a short introduction, and a Heading 2 followed by a short list.
  4. Add a descriptive link, such as Experience Workspace authoring documentation, rather than "click here".
  5. If your configured library contains an approved template, you can insert it from the tools panel and replace the sample copy. Templates available to you depend on the site configuration.

Preview your draft

  1. Allow your changes to finish saving. Check any save or connection indicator in your editor.
  2. Open the Send menu. Choose Preview, not Preview & Publish, and select Update.
  3. Open the preview URL. Check your heading, list, and link on a narrow screen as well as a wider one.
  4. Change one sentence in the editor and preview again. Confirm the new sentence appears.

In the earlier document editor, use its Preview action or the Sidekick. See Preview and publish safely for the distinction between preview and live.

Check your result

  • The page has one clear Heading 1.
  • Headings describe the sections beneath them.
  • The link opens the intended destination.
  • Your latest change appears at the preview URL.
  • You have not published the practice page.

Source: Authoring pages and Publishing content.
